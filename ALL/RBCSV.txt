RBCSV EXPLANATION:

- Two sensors pointing on either side of the road, measuring the angle of the road
- RBCSV Value is a delta of these, there are a total of 4 sensors (the ones determining RBCSV_60 and the ones determining RBCSV_80)
- Roads tend to come up in the middle with time, making the shape they are always built in more extreme
- This measures at what angle the roads go down on the sides
